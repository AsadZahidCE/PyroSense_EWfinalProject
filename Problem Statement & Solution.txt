Problem Statement
Fire and gas leakages are significant safety hazards in both residential and industrial environments. Conventional fire alarms often suffer from several limitations:
* Localized Alerts Only: Traditional alarms only provide a sound at the physical location. If the occupants are away from home, they have no way of knowing a fire has started until it is too late.
* Single-Sensor Reliance: Many basic systems detect either only smoke or only heat. This leads to slower response times for fast-burning fires that produce minimal smoke initially.
* Lack of Real-Time Monitoring: There is no way to monitor environmental safety levels remotely or receive digital records of safety breaches.
In the context of modern engineering, there is a critical need for an interconnected system that not only alerts local occupants but also notifies remote users via cloud-based platforms.
Proposed Solution: PyroSense
To address these challenges, I have developed PyroSense, an IoT-integrated safety system built on the ESP32 platform. This project serves as a comprehensive solution by combining multi-sensor detection with cloud connectivity.
Key Features of the Solution:
1. Dual-Sensor Detection Architecture:
   * I integrated the MQ-2 Smoke/Gas Sensor to monitor for smoldering fires and gas leaks (LPG/Propane).
   * I incorporated the IR Flame Sensor to detect the infrared spectrum of open flames, ensuring immediate detection of high-intensity fires.
2. Tiered Alert System:
   * Local Alert: A high-decibel active buzzer and a flashing Red LED provide immediate feedback to anyone nearby.
   * Cloud Alert: Utilizing the Blynk IoT framework, the system pushes real-time notifications to the user's smartphone and sends an automated emergency email.
3. Visual Status Monitoring:
   * The system features a "Heartbeat" indicator (Green LED) to confirm the device is online and the environment is safe, fulfilling the requirement for a user-friendly interface.
4. Low-Power IoT Implementation:
   * By using the ESP32 and Wi-Fi protocols (concepts learned in EL1006 Lab 13), the solution remains cost-effective, scalable, and easy to install in any Wi-Fi-enabled environment.
Summary of the Engineering Approach
Instead of a simple "Beep-only" alarm, PyroSense transforms fire safety into a data-driven IoT application. By mapping physical hazards to digital events, the proposed solution minimizes response time, potentially saving lives and reducing property damage through early intervention.
