PyroSense: IoT-Based Fire & Gas Safety System
PyroSense is an intelligent safety solution designed to provide real-time environmental monitoring. By leveraging the power of the ESP32 and Blynk IoT, it ensures that homeowners are notified of potential hazards the moment they occur.
 Component List
1. The Core Controller
* ESP32 DevKit V1: The central processing unit featuring integrated Wi-Fi. It manages sensor data processing and handles the encrypted connection to the Blynk Cloud.
2. Detection Suite
* MQ-2 Gas/Smoke Sensor: A high-sensitivity sensor capable of detecting LPG, Propane, Hydrogen, and Smoke particles. It provides real-time analog data for precise threshold monitoring.
* IR Flame Detection Module: An infrared-based sensor that identifies the spectral signature of fire. It provides a high-speed digital trigger for instant fire detection.
3. Alert & Notification Hardware
* Active Piezo Buzzer: Provides a high-decibel audible alarm during emergency states.
* Dual-LED Visual Indicators:
   * Crimson Red LED: Dedicated for "Danger Mode" (flashing alert).
   * Emerald Green LED: Dedicated for "Safe Mode" (steady heartbeat).
* Current Limiting Resistors (220Ω): Precision resistors to maintain LED longevity and prevent voltage spikes.
4. Prototyping & Power
* PyroSense Breadboard Hub: A 830-point mounting station for all components.
* Jumper Wire Harness: A mix of Male-to-Male and Male-to-Female wires for low-resistance signal paths.
* Micro-USB Power Interface: Provides a stable 5V DC supply to the system.

 System Architecture (Software)
