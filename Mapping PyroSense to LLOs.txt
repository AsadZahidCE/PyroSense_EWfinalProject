Lab Learning Outcomes (LLOs)  Achievement
This project is a practical application of the outcomes defined in the EL1006 syllabus:
LLO 2: System Assembly and Circuit Construction
* Guided Hands-on Activity: I successfully assembled the electronic circuit and network components. This fulfills the requirements of Lab 5 (System Assembly) and Labs 8-11 (Circuit Construction).
* Networking: By configuring the ESP32 to communicate with the "Fast Faculty" Wi-Fi and the Blynk IoT server, I applied the networking concepts introduced in Lab 13.
PLO-01: Engineering Knowledge
* I applied fundamental engineering principles to calibrate the smoke threshold and manage the logic states (HIGH/LOW) of the sensors to solve the real-world problem of fire safety.
PLO-05: Modern Tool Usage
* I utilized modern engineering and IT tools, specifically the Arduino IDE for programming, Blynk IoT for cloud-based monitoring, and GitHub for version control and documentation.
